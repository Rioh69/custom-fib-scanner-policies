Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Custom Fib Scanner Alerts, a private SMS alert system used by the sole proprietor/user to receive market scanner notifications on their own mobile phone.

Information We Collect

We collect the mobile phone number that is opted in to receive SMS alerts. Scanner alert messages may include ticker symbols that meet completed review criteria.

How We Use Information

The opted-in mobile phone number is used only to send low-volume SMS alerts when the private scanner detects completed review criteria. The SMS alert prompts the user to check Discord for full details.

How Users Opt In

The sole proprietor/user opts in by texting START from their own mobile phone to the Twilio phone number. Messages are sent only to the opted-in mobile number.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opt Out

You can opt out of SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for help.

Contact

For help with Custom Fib Scanner Alerts, reply HELP to the SMS number.
